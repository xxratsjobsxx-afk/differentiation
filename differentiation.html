1 rad All angles on this page are in radians. The derivative of sin is cos only when angles are measured in radians: the proof leans on the limit limh→0 (sin h)/h = 1, which is true in radians and false in degrees. In degrees the derivative would come out as (π/180) cos x. One radian is the angle that cuts an arc equal to the radius: 1 rad ≈ 57.3°, and a full turn is 2π radians.
